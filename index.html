Deep Dive: What are Lexical Chunks?

Groups of words processed by the brain as a single unit of meaning, bypassing grammatical analysis.

Traditional

Building from scratch:

"I" + "have" + "been" + "learning"

High cognitive load. Slow processing.

Lexical

Plugging in blocks:

[I've been learning...] + [topic] + [for X years]

Low cognitive load. Instant fluency.

Active Participation

Challenge: Designing the Lexical Club

1. Group Up

Form small teams of 3-4 people.

2. Invent a Location

e.g., Bowling alley, museum, supermarket.

3. Extract Chunks

Select 3-5 core lexical chunks for the activity.

Be prepared to pitch your idea and explain how the environment naturally forces the use of these phrases!

The Chunk Hunt & Talk

Outdoor Application Activity

  • 1
    The Index Card: Take a card with 3 functional chunks (e.g., "Speaking of...", "To be honest...").
  • 2
    The Walk: We are heading outside. Maintain a natural conversation.
  • 3
    The Goal: Seamlessly drop all three of your chunks into the conversation.
Lowers the affective filter!
